# Landmark Research Papers in Generative AI

A curated list of foundational research papers that shaped modern AI and Large Language Models.

* **[Attention Is All You Need (Transformer Architecture)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)** - The seminal paper introducing the Transformer model.
* **[Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (GPT-3)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165)** - Demonstrating the power of in-context learning scaling.
* **[Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401)** - The foundational paper introducing RAG.
